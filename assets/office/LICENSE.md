# 3D office assets

These pinned LOD1 GLB files are from [tevy.dev](https://tevy.dev) and are released under **CC0 1.0**:

- Meeting Room v2 — `https://tevy.dev/models/meeting-room/v2/lod1.glb`
- Office Cubicle Pod v2 — `https://tevy.dev/models/cubicle-pod/v2/lod1.glb`
- Office Reception v2 — `https://tevy.dev/models/reception-desk/v2/lod1.glb`

They provide real desks, monitors, chairs, glass partitions, a meeting table, screen, doors and reception furniture. They are kept locally for GitHub Pages deployment.
